    The ritual

    Morning or night.
    About a minute.

    1. 01
      Three drops

      Onto clean skin that is still a little damp.

    2. 02
      Warm them

      Between your fingertips, until you can smell the heather.

    3. 03
      Press, don't rub

      Cheeks, jaw, throat. Hold for a breath and let the skin drink.
